What I recommend

Instead of building a simple video sync application, build it like a mini distributed real-time system.

The goal should be:

"A production-inspired real-time synchronization platform that maintains a single authoritative playback timeline while multiple display clients continuously synchronize themselves under varying network conditions."

That sounds exactly like how Netflix, YouTube Watch Party, digital signage systems, and stadium displays work.
